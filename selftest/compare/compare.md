## Reading the numbers

Online is usually more fluent on standard prose. The offline path is deterministic once the model is loaded, keeps text on this machine, and follows the curated phrasebook for known slang and dialect expressions. Its weakness is vocabulary and dialect coverage outside that table, and CPU latency is much higher.

This set is small and the references are authored for Qalam, so treat the differences as directional. Use the visible outputs above to judge register, not only the F1 column.
